Questions, answered

Straight answers, including what VitaPulse can’t do yet.

Is VitaPulse HIPAA compliant?

Not yet. VitaPulse is a demonstration workspace and has not been certified or audited for HIPAA. Please use sample data only until your practice has reviewed its compliance needs.

Does e-Prescribe send prescriptions to real pharmacies?

Not yet. You can write and track prescriptions and check them against a patient’s allergies and current medications, but VitaPulse is not certified e-prescribing software and does not transmit to pharmacies.

Is the telehealth video private?

Each appointment gets its own video link, and anyone with the link can join, so share it only with the patient. The video service is not HIPAA certified.

Can my whole team use it?

Yes. The clinic manager creates the clinic and invites practitioners and staff from the Team page.

What can I attach to a patient?

A photo, an ID and an insurance card (images or PDFs, up to 5 MB each), plus forms and chart notes.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to phones, tablets and computers.

e-Prescribe

The pharmacy field is for your own records — picking "CVS" or "Walgreens" here doesn't send anything to them. The allergy/duplicate-therapy check above is a simple keyword match against this chart, not real clinical decision support — it can miss things and isn't a substitute for judgment. VitaPulse isn't connected to any pharmacy's real system, a payer, or a certified drug-interaction database, and isn't certified e-prescribing software. Route anything that actually needs to reach a pharmacy, or any controlled substance, through your certified e-prescribing system instead.
