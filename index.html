e-Prescribe

The pharmacy field is for your own records — picking "CVS" or "Walgreens" here doesn't send anything to them. The allergy/duplicate-therapy check above is a simple keyword match against this chart, not real clinical decision support — it can miss things and isn't a substitute for judgment. VitaPulse isn't connected to any pharmacy's real system, a payer, or a certified drug-interaction database, and isn't certified e-prescribing software. Route anything that actually needs to reach a pharmacy, or any controlled substance, through your certified e-prescribing system instead.
